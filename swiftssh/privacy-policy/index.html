Privacy Policy for SwiftSSH

Last Updated: April 16, 2026

SwiftSSH (“we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy. This policy describes how we handle information in connection with the SwiftSSH iOS application. The app is not yet published; we will update this page if practices change before or after launch. Structure is consistent with our other apps (for example LinkManager).

1. Summary

  • Connection profiles, keys, and session-related data you enter are intended to stay on your device unless a feature explicitly exports or syncs data (only if you enable it).
  • We do not sell SSH credentials or terminal content for advertising.
  • Optional diagnostics (e.g. crash logs) depend on what is compiled into the release and your device settings.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide

Host names, ports, usernames, passwords or key material, session preferences, and similar data you save in the app. Storage location and encryption follow the implementation in the shipped build (e.g. secure storage APIs on iOS).

2.2 Technical and diagnostic data

If crash reporting or analytics SDKs are included, we may receive limited technical data (e.g. crash traces, app version, device model, OS version). What is collected depends on the release you install and your system privacy choices.

2.3 Network activity

SSH connections are made to servers you configure. We do not route your SSH traffic through our servers as part of normal app operation unless a future feature clearly discloses otherwise.

3. How we use information

  • To provide SSH, terminal, and related features
  • To improve stability and fix bugs
  • To comply with law where required

4. Sharing

We do not sell your connection profiles or terminal output. We may share information only when:

  • You export or share data via system features to a destination you choose
  • Required by law or valid legal process
  • Service providers process limited data under contract (e.g. crash tooling), consistent with the shipped build

5. Security

We rely on iOS platform security and careful handling of credentials in the app. You should use strong passwords, protect private keys, and keep the OS updated.

6. Children’s privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected on this page and, where appropriate, in the app.
