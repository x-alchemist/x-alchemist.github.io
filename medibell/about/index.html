Overview

DoseChime is an iOS medication reminder app: separate profiles for family members, medications and schedules, local notifications, and calendar/history to help doses stay on time. Core data is stored on your device; backup and export depend on features you use and where you save files.

Feature summary

  • Multiple profiles, medications, and dosing rules
  • Local notifications and a today list (taken / skipped / missed)
  • Calendar, history, inventory, and low-stock cues
  • Theme & language, local backup/restore where provided

Important: The app is for reminders and logging only—not a substitute for your clinician or pharmacist.
